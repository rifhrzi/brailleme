# Pinned DSBI TRAIN assessment

The standalone parser passes all 26 downloaded TRAIN sources and eight focused unit tests. The census contains 9,983 nonzero cells, 83 explicit zero-mask cells, and 26,590 occupied dots. All 63 nonzero patterns occur, although mask 40 has only one example and mask 63 has thirteen. There are four metadata source families: Massage (10 pages, including one empty recto annotation), Math (10), Ordinary Printed Document (3), and Shaver Yang Fengting (3). The 88 TEST images were not downloaded or opened.

All 55 downloaded files match the pinned download manifest. All grid coordinates and all expanded source boxes fit their supplied deskewed RGB images. The parser rejects malformed/nonfinite headers, invalid grid sizes/order, nonbinary bits, out-of-range indices and duplicate row/column entries. It preserves one-based row/column identity and six column-major bits; mask zero remains explicit background and cannot be presented as an A–Z target.

The cell rectangle uses the full 2×3 grid, including unoccupied positions, expanded by 0.3 horizontal dot-column pitches per edge. The pinned [AngelinaReader caller](https://github.com/IlyaOvodov/AngelinaReader/blob/328b0e0d30353d76ec06cde8560a876da5c46ab7/data_utils/data.py#L279-L282) supplies that default to its [DSBI reader](https://github.com/IlyaOvodov/AngelinaReader/blob/328b0e0d30353d76ec06cde8560a876da5c46ab7/data_utils/dsbi.py#L98-L105). This produces a median box aspect ratio of 0.61538 (5th–95th percentiles 0.56929–0.64646). It is a documented geometric envelope, not a measured segmentation of every optical highlight or shadow. Source boxes remain unclipped. The actual middle horizontal grid line is preserved: 7,479 cell entries differ from an arithmetic top/bottom midpoint, by up to three source pixels; the optional upstream point-mode midpoint shortcut is not used.

The coordinate space is **the provided `+recto.jpg` after dataset deskewing**. Our image transform is identity: decode that JPEG as RGB; apply no rotation, expansion, fill, or resampling before context cropping. The annotation angle is retained as provenance only. The [pinned DSBI README](https://github.com/yeluo1994/DSBI/blob/6937eb8973efe5ff337e5c018485cb626303f5c2/README.md) separately gives a raw-image OpenCV recipe: call `imageRotate` with the negative recorded angle, use center `(width/2,height/2)`, expand from absolute sine/cosine bounds, add `cvRound` offsets, and use linear interpolation with a constant white border. That optional raw recipe was not executed or pixel-verified here; the original skewed training JPEGs were not part of this download. Future `crop_transform` must receive the deskewed RGB and these matching coordinates.

AI visual review covered twelve positive cells, selected before any inference by a deterministic sparse/dense rule across the four families, four explicit blank cells, and the complete image with an empty recto annotation. Occupied grid positions qualitatively correspond to the visible front-side dots, including the full empty-column/row extent of sparse cells. No blocking alignment or mask fault was observed. Local paper distortion and bright/dark lobes make some optical centers approximate; this is not a certification of every dot or every footprint pixel. The empty annotation is the documented no-recto-side case, not an assumption that its image lacks paper marks or verso impressions.

The [DSBI paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10893) describes 200-dpi flatbed scans and automated annotation initialization followed by human correction. It does not establish the accuracy of this model on handheld photographs. It also does not independently certify every one of the 12,579 unlisted grid slots in this local assessment. For the authorized append-only preparation, those unlisted slots will therefore become separate `ignore_boxes`, never positive cells; explicit mask-zero entries remain background. No training manifest was changed during this assessment.

An exact JPEG byte-hash check found no overlap between these 26 DSBI sources and the 290 original Angelina train/validation/external sources. This does not rule out transformed or recompressed copies. Detailed hashes, family/mask counts, source dimensions and review crops are in `train-assessment.json`, `download-manifest.json`, `angelina-exact-source-overlap.json`, and `grid-contact-*.png`.
