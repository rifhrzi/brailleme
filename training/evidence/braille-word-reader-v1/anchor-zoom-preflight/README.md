# Run28 anchor zoom preflight

This archive binds the actual run28 configuration, protocol, 23 source files and 71 source-bound prelaunch tests. The prototype and independent proof exercise geometry and training targets with synthetic RGB and 40 fixed TRAIN metadata records over 32 diagnostic phases. They do not measure recognition accuracy or actual training-epoch coverage.

The nearest originally complete known cell can cap the existing affine zoom. No new RNG or raster is added; visibility stays irreversible and later rotation can still cut a cell. The v1 float32 boundary defect and separate earlier mistaken test expectation are retained under historical-v1. Only the corrected v2 helper was trained.

preflight.json maps every source/report role to exact repository-relative bytes. Identical source bytes are stored once. paired-record-results.json.gz expands losslessly to the recorded raw SHA-256. Large immutable training manifests and DSBI point metadata are linked to existing data-manifests archives, not duplicated. Original reports retain their original paths and claims. No model binaries, source photographs, validation predictions or training-completion claim are included.

archive-index.json hashes every new payload including preflight.json. The stdlib retainer verifies original bindings, gzip contents and copied bytes; it refuses to overwrite this archive.
